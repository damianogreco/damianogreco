    Research Interests

    My main interest is the analysis of Nonlinear Partial Differential Equations. During my doctoral studies I focused on Thomas-Fermi type nonlocal variational problems using tools from PDEs, calculus of variations, and potential theory. Recently, I have moved my attention to nonlinear dispersive equations. Namely, by also employing techniques from probability theory, I have been studying well-posedness (existence and uniqueness of solutions) as well as existence and properties of associated invariant (Gibbs) measures.
